Recipe Website
Welcome to the recipe website! This is an application designed for college students to help you find the perfect recipes! Whether you want to lose weight, eat for fitness, or just want to find a new recipe to try, this website has you covered! Use the navigation bar above to explore the different features of the website.
Recipe Explorer
Here you can search for new recipes to add to your favorites! You can search for recipes by name, ingredients, or even by dietary restrictions.
User Recipes
Here you can view all of your favorite recipes! You can also remove recipes from your favorites list if you no longer want them.
